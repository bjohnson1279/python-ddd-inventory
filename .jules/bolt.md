## 2025-02-18 - [Bolt Optimization] O(1) Ledger Aggregate Indexing
**Learning:** In event-sourced architectures (like `ComplianceLedger`), retrieving events for a specific aggregate or reconstructing state involves full O(N) list scans of the entire event history. As the ledger grows, this becomes a severe bottleneck.
**Action:** Always introduce an internal hash map (e.g., `defaultdict(list)`) mapping `aggregate_id` to its corresponding events. This converts O(N) list scans into O(1) index lookups, drastically reducing state reconstruction and retrieval times. Ensure that any methods returning internal collections return a copy (`list(...)` or `dict(...)`) to prevent external callers from mutating the internal index.
